# Official Translations of the Tie Public License / Tie Public License 官方翻译

## Designation / 指定

Under **Section 15.8** of the Tie Public License, the Licensor may publish
translations and may designate a translation as an **"Official Translation"**.

The **TIE-LANG organization**, as the author and publisher of the canonical text
of the Tie Public License (`tpl.txt` in this repository), hereby designates the
translations listed below as **Official Translations** of the current version
of the License.

依据 **Tie Public License 第 15.8 条**，许可人可发布译文，并可指定某译文为
**「官方翻译」**。

**TIE-LANG 组织**作为 Tie Public License 权威文本（本仓库 `tpl.txt`）的作者与
发布方，特此指定下列译文为当前版本许可证的**官方翻译**。

Date of designation / 指定日期: **2026-09-29**

## Effect / 效力

1. **Precedence of English / 英文优先** — Consistent with Section 15.8, the
   conditions of the License apply equally to all language versions, and in the
   event of any conflict or inconsistency between an Official Translation and
   the English text, **the English text prevails**.
   与第 15.8 条一致：许可证条件平等适用于所有语言版本；官方翻译与英文文本发生
   任何冲突或不一致时，**以英文文本为准**。

2. **Purpose / 用途** — Official Translations are published to aid
   understanding of the License for readers in each language community. They are
   reference renderings; they do not create obligations or rights beyond those
   of the English text.
   官方翻译用于帮助各语言读者理解本许可证，属参考性表述，不产生超出英文文本的
   义务或权利。

3. **Scope of this designation / 本指定的范围** — This designation is made by
   the TIE-LANG organization with respect to the text of the Tie Public License
   itself. A person who adopts the Tie Public License for their own work is a
   Licensor in their own right and may separately designate translations of
   their own notices and documentation.
   本指定由 TIE-LANG 组织就 Tie Public License 文本本身作出。采用本许可证授权
   自己作品的人是自己的许可人，可另行指定其自身通知与文档的译文。

## Revision and errata / 修订与勘误

Translations remain subject to correction. Where an Official Translation is
found to depart from the English text, the English text governs, and the
translation will be corrected in a subsequent revision. Reports may be filed as
an issue in this repository.

译文可能被更正。若发现官方翻译偏离英文文本，以英文文本为准，该译文将在后续修订
中更正。可通过本仓库的 issue 报告。

A new version of the License (for example TPL 2.4) requires a new designation;
the designations recorded here apply to the versions stated in the table above.

许可证换版（例如 TPL 2.4）需重新指定；本文件所载指定仅适用于上表所列版本。
